# Choice Formats: Multiple Choice, Multi-Select, True/False

**Status:** research synthesis, 4 Nov 2026; pending owner decision.

## Bottom line

Multiple choice is a **good** format when the options make players look or think, and every item is corrected. True/false is useful only for confronting one misconception, with an immediate correction. Neither should be used as routine trivia.

## Multiple choice (`ANS-MC1`)

| Finding | Label |
|---|---|
| Plausible, competitive wrong options trigger real retrieval and help recall of related facts | OBSERVED: [Little et al. 2012](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2012/10/121005123902.htm) |
| More wrong options → more wrong answers produced later | OBSERVED: [Roediger & Marsh 2005](https://profiles.wustl.edu/en/publications/the-positive-and-negative-consequences-of-multiple-choice-testing/) |
| Feedback removes most of that harm | OBSERVED: Butler & Roediger 2008, via [Learning Scientists](https://www.learningscientists.org/blog/2018/8/2) |
| Multiple choice vs very-short-answer practice: **no retention difference**; first-try success 73.9% vs 58.6% | OBSERVED: [van Wijk et al. 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11684041) (45 medical students, no feedback) |
| Multiple choice helped in all 7 classroom studies reviewed, including 6th grade | OBSERVED: [Moreira et al. 2019](https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/feduc.2019.00005) |
| Children aged 4–9 in a museum: retrieval practice improved one-week **recognition** but not free recall | OBSERVED (title verified; details as reported by the GPT run): "Memory for a dinosaur exhibit: retrieval-based practice vs. restudy", PubMed 42401060 |

**Rules (INFERRED):**
- 3 options, all plausible.
- Options that can only be told apart on site.
- Always reveal and explain.
- Avoid a deliberately funny but false option that stays in memory.

## Multi-select (`ANS-MCN`)

- **UNKNOWN** for learning; no direct study found.
- Fits "which of these can you see from here?" observation checks (INFERRED).
- Actionbound supports partial points ("Rate Loosely"); Loquiz and Adventure Lab support multi-select (OBSERVED).

## True/false (`ANS-TF`)

| Finding | Label |
|---|---|
| True/false review beat rereading. True-only items didn't transfer. Correcting false items helped only with feedback | OBSERVED: [Uner, Tekin & Roediger 2021](https://profiles.wustl.edu/en/publications/truefalse-tests-enhance-retention-relative-to-rerea/) |
| "Truth-checking" helped in only some experiments, sometimes made people remember false information as true, and took longer | OBSERVED: [Arcos, Hausman & Storm 2023](https://hausman.sites.ucsc.edu/?p=279) |

**Conflict:** the Claude run proposed "fact or legend?" as a natural DORI fit. The GPT run warns it can make the legend more memorable than the correction. **Merged rule (INFERRED):** use true/false rarely, include false items, ask players to correct the false one, and show the correction immediately and clearly. Don't use it for ordinary facts. Chance of a correct guess is 50%.
