# Photo, Video and Attention

**Status:** research synthesis, 4 Nov 2026; pending owner decision. Verification methods: see `Run_10b_photo_answer_verification.md`.

## Bottom line

Photography helps learning **when it tells players what to look at**, and hurts when it replaces looking. Keep photo tasks apart from narration.

## Evidence

| Finding | Label |
|---|---|
| Photographing whole objects lowered memory for objects, details and locations; **zooming in on a detail removed the impairment** (art-museum tour) | OBSERVED: [Henkel 2013](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/news/releases/no-pictures-please-taking-photos-may-impede-memory-of-museum-tour.html) |
| Photo-takers recognised more visual information but remembered less audio; the effect held with mere intent to photograph (one field study, three lab studies) | OBSERVED: [Barasch et al. 2017](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/news/releases/taking-photos-of-experiences-boosts-visual-memory-impairs-auditory-memory.html) |
| Media answers are almost never auto-checked: Actionbound missions "do not check whether the result is correct"; Scavify awards photo points on upload; Loquiz instructors review | OBSERVED: [Actionbound](https://en.actionbound.com/help/article/mission); [Scavify](https://scavify.com/howitworks-task-types); [Loquiz](https://loquiz.com/support/feature-list/) |
| Video learning benefit specific to heritage walks | UNKNOWN |

## Good photo tasks (INFERRED)

- Zoom in on one detail (an emblem, a date, a carving).
- Recreate an archival viewpoint (rephotography). The photo becomes a souvenir and is not scored.
- Find an example of a category ("any carved motif").
- Creative composition (hands or shadow with a mural); participation only, never scored.

## Weak photo tasks

- "Take a picture here."
- Photographing the whole building.
- Photo tasks during a guide video or audio.
- Scoring how beautiful a photo is.

## Timing rule (INFERRED)

Visual search first, narration after, or the reverse. **Never at the same time.**

## Unknown

- Whether rephotography improves memory of architectural change compared with viewing a then-and-now pair (test: two conditions, delayed recognition).
- How many photo tasks per route before photography takes over (test: 1 vs 3).
