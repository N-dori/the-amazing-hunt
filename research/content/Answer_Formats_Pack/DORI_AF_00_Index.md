## Ten decision rules (proposals)

1. **Every scored answer ends in a reveal** that gives the right answer and ties it to what players can see. A bare tick is a wasted moment. → 01
2. **Commit first, then reveal.** Guess, choose or estimate before the explanation. → 01, 04
3. **Multiple choice: 3 plausible options, always corrected.** More options plant more wrong answers. → 02
4. **True/false sparingly,** only for one misconception at a time, with an immediate correction. → 02
5. **Typed answers only when the word is visible on site or easy to produce,** and always through the tiered checker with feedback. → 03
6. **For history with children, ordering images beats dates.** Follow it with a "why did it change?" prompt. → 04
7. **Photo tasks say what to notice:** a detail, an archival viewpoint or a category. Never "photograph this", and never during narration. → 05
8. **Make the family look at one thing together** (point, count, tap the spot) before answering. → 06
9. **Polls and votes are for perspective, not scoring;** use them to start talk before a reveal. → 06
10. **Pick the format for the learning job, not for variety.** → 07
11. **A route-long collection:** introduce it once, use varied examples, add one new fact per find, and end with a compare step. Lions overlap a competitor, menorahs are seasonal, and tzedakah boxes need a scouting walk first. → 09
12. **Window to the past:** a parallax archival photo you look around by tilting the phone. Stand still, keep it under 30 seconds, pair it with "what's gone?", and label anything invented. → 10

## Biggest open questions (cheapest test)

| # | Question | Test |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Does "guess, then reveal" beat "reveal, then quiz" for families outdoors? | Paper A/B, 3 stops, 10 families per arm, next-day recall |
| 2 | Do "look together" tasks raise family talk compared with multiple choice? | Record 6 families (with consent); code joint attention |
| 3 | Can children aged 6–10 type answers on a phone in the street? | Timing test with 10 children |
| 4 | Is the tiered checker accurate on real family answers? | Score 200 typed answers against human judgement |
| 5 | Does ordering three images teach *why*, or only *when*? | Add one delayed "why did this change?" question |
| 6 | How many photo tasks per route before photography takes over? | Routes with 1 vs 3 photo tasks; narration recall and enjoyment |
| 7 | Does a family vote before the reveal improve recall or perspective-taking? | A/B: vote vs one person taps |
| 8 | Are enough tzedakah boxes visible on the route? | One scouting walk with GPS and a photo per box |
| 9 | Does a parallax past-view beat a flat then/now pair for recall? | A/B at one stop; build version B from one Matson photo |
