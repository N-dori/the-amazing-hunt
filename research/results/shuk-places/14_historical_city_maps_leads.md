**What this is:** that research looked for maps of the **Old City and Jaffa Gate**, not of the Shuk's area. This file keeps only the city-scale maps and collections that **might** reach west of the walls, as leads for a possible then-and-now challenge (e.g. the market area before the covered market, or the courtyard neighbourhoods as first drawn). **No row was checked for Nahlaot or Mahane Yehuda.** Every coverage line below is open until someone opens the map. ## Rights rules from that research

- An NLI item-level "Public Domain Free Use" statement counts, not NLI's site-wide terms; NLI's written confirmation is still open (platform open item 2d) and is needed only before a map is **shown** in the app.
- "Subject to copyright" or a modern reconstruction: facts only, never traced or copied.
- Wikimedia Commons tags are user-applied: trace each file back to its holding institution.
- Israeli state works made in 1976 or later are still in copyright (Copyright Act 2007, §42: 50 years from making).
- Israel State Archives' terms contradict themselves on commercial use and forbid derivatives: get written confirmation first.
- BnF (Gallica) counts an app as commercial use, which is paid.

## Not a source for the navigation map

These are reference pictures (Type A in [the map-types spec](../../../docs/design/map-types-spec.md)). The Shuk's navigation map is Type B: its geometry comes from OpenStreetMap, never from a historical or copyrighted map.
