## Scope and editorial rule
This revised concept uses English youth/internet/gaming expressions whose mainstream prominence falls roughly within **2011–2026**, while preserving easy-to-say market-food identities. A name need not be entirely slang: familiar food words and conventional grammar remain allowed. The objective is contemporary *tone*, not a museum of slang, and not forced Gen Alpha meme-speak. **Falafel Fugitives** is deliberately preserved as a strong evergreen benchmark despite not depending on recent slang.

The 2022–2025 Oxford Word of the Year record supports the recent prominence of *goblin mode* (2022), *rizz* (2023), *brain rot* (2024), and *aura farming* (2025 shortlist); Cambridge defines *no cap*; Merriam-Webster documents *vibe check*. Not all documented slang is suitable: *rizz* frequently has romantic implications; *brain rot*, *rage bait*, *skibidi*, and overly ephemeral memes are excluded from the core bank. “Main character,” “plot twist,” “side quest,” “boss mode,” “level up,” “glow up,” “hype,” and “low-key” are modern-pop-culture naming devices, but **not claimed to have been coined within the period**. These references establish recent prominence, not a precise origin date for each expression.

### Why the top set works
A mix of action (“Fugitives,” “on the Run”), contemporary social language (“Vibe Check,” “Glow Up,” “No Cap”), playful game language (“Side Quest,” “Boss Mode”), and food-personality humor. “Falafel Fugitives” is retained because it is exceptionally easy to chant, recognizable internationally, and visually strong without requiring anyone to know a meme.

## Pronunciation and language review
- **Falafel Fugitives:** fuh-LAH-fel; joke: Falafel balls escaped the pita. Hebrew `הפלאפלים הנמלטים`; alliteration lost.
- **Sabich Side Quest:** SAH-beekh; joke: Sabich has a surprise side mission. Hebrew `משימת הסביח`; side quest is game jargon; adapt.
- **Rugelach on the Run:** ROO-guh-lakh; joke: Pastries outrun the baker. Hebrew `הרוגלך במנוסה`; natural paraphrase.
- **Bourekas Boss Mode:** boo-REH-kas; joke: Flaky pastry becomes the final boss. Hebrew `בורקס במצב בוס`; gaming expression localized.
- **Tahini Vibe Check:** tuh-HEE-nee; joke: The sesame sauce judges the team vibe. Hebrew `מבחן הווייב של הטחינה`; vibe borrowed into Hebrew.
- **Kubbeh Chaos Crew:** KOO-beh; joke: Soup dumplings form a chaotic crew. Hebrew `חבורת בלגן הקובה`; crew idiom adapted.
- **Halva Main Character:** HAHL-vah; joke: Halva insists it is the star. Hebrew `החלבה גונבת את ההצגה`; main-character slang paraphrased.
- **Knafeh Unfiltered:** kuh-NAH-feh; joke: The sticky dessert has zero interest in posing. Hebrew `כנאפה בלי פילטר`; unfiltered works bilingually.
- **Pickle Plot Twist:** PIK-ul; joke: The pickles unexpectedly change the story. Hebrew `טוויסט החמוצים`; plot twist is borrowed.
- **Olive Squad Goals:** OL-iv; joke: A team of olives achieves perfect coordination. Hebrew `נבחרת הזיתים`; slang sense lost.
- **Pomegranate Glow Up:** POM-uh-gran-it; joke: A plain fruit becomes a ruby-red celebrity. Hebrew `הרימון שהשתדרג`; glow up paraphrased.
- **Baklava No Cap:** bahk-lah-VAH; joke: This pastry really believes it is unbeatable. Hebrew `בקלאווה בלי להגזים`; no cap slang untranslatable.

Localize by **stable name ID**, not English text. Hebrew may paraphrase a joke rather than directly copy slang. A Hebrew language reviewer should validate register, number, orthography and pronunciation; check family audiences and Hebrew–English mixed groups. Terms such as *no cap*, *squad goals*, and *plot armor* are weaker in translation; prioritize them only when the icon communicates the joke.

## One-phone-screen onboarding proposal
1. Leader joins or creates their team first, so the server has a durable `team_id`.
2. Show six **available** character cards (two columns × three rows), each with avatar and 1–2-line English label; “See more names” opens the full approved bank.
3. A tap selects locally, but **Confirm name** requests a server-side claim. Never represent local selection as secured.
4. On success, show a brief confirmation and populate Group sheet, Host overview, invitations, leaderboard and relevant mission headings from the same name ID.
5. On conflict, say “Another team just claimed that name—pick another Shuk legend!” and refresh availability.

## Fixed bank implementation specification (no code)
- `name_catalog`: stable `name_id`, English/Hebrew display labels, food reference, avatar asset, sort/featured rank, enabled flag and catalog version.
- `teams`: stable `team_id`, `adventure_id`, leader reference and claimed `name_id`.
- **Uniqueness invariant:** per active adventure, `(adventure_id, name_id)` can be claimed by at most one team; different adventures may reuse all names.
- Enforce the invariant in a **single server transaction plus database unique constraint** (or equivalent transactional guarantee). Client-side disabling alone is never authoritative.
- Make retries idempotent. If a client times out after commit, its next request should discover its own successful claim, not allocate a second name.
- Catalog changes may not retroactively alter an already-running adventure’s labels; use snapshot/version retention.
- Define owner-only name release/change policy and audit any reassignment. Rejoining leaders see the existing name.
- Test simultaneous same-name claims, lost acknowledgements, reconnects, stale availability, team deletion, catalog revisions and localization.

## Owner review before approval
- **Slang durability:** consider swapping “No Cap” or “Squad Goals” for a timeless alternate if the primary audience spans many generations.
- **Authenticity:** verify specialist foods against the actual planned market route and vendors; avoid stating Israeli origin simply because a food is available there.
- **Cultural nuance:** jokes are about imaginary food characters, never about ethnic groups, religious practices or accents.
- **Family tone:** avoid romantic *rizz*, derogatory *brain rot*, violent *rage bait*, offensive memes and brand-adjacent restaurant names.
- **Rights:** names are proposals, not trademark-cleared. Final avatar art should be original and not reuse restaurant logos.
- **Hebrew:** native speaker proofreading is required before launch; variants in this file are creative drafts, not verified translations.
