# Israeli Mosaic UX

The opening screen is an editorial mosaic that invites the visitor to notice and choose a social lens. The primary invitation is conceptually “Who catches your eye?”

## First vertical slice

- Responsive mosaic landing screen.
- Approximately 8–10 visible entries on a tablet viewport, with more available by scrolling.
- Accessible keyboard and touch interaction.
- Lens detail state with optional, sourced content.
- Minimal `choose → add → add → break the box` overlap interaction.
- No politics in the first screen or first reveal.

## Interaction rules

- Selecting a lens must not imply that it fully describes a person.
- Adding a lens should preserve the previously selected lenses.
- The reveal should communicate: **these are overlapping lenses, not boxes**.
- Reduced-motion users receive the same state change without animated travel.
- RTL and LTR must be considered from the beginning.

The approved synthesis is preserved in `docs/Israeli_Mosaic_UX_UI_Synthesis_Brief.md`.
