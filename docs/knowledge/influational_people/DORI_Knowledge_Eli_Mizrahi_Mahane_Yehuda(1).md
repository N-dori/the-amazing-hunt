# Eli Mizrahi — A person behind the changing market

> **Editorial boundary:** This is a sourced background file for DORI's `docs/knowledge/` folder, **not** an approved challenge, biography script, or visitor-facing memorial. Public statements about Eli Mizrahi are attributed to their speakers. Distinguish what is documented, what someone remembered or praised, and what DORI proposes as interpretation.

## 1. Identity and relevance

- **Name:** אלי מזרחי — Eli Mizrahi.
- **Dates:** Died on **25 May 2022**, reported aged 70. Exact birth date not established here. [S1, S2]
- **Connection to place:** Entrepreneur and former chair of the Mahane Yehuda Market merchants' committee. [S1–S3]
- **Key development:** Opened **Café Mizrahi** inside Mahane Yehuda in **2002**, when a café was unusual among the market's traditional produce and food stalls. The café became associated with the area's shift toward restaurants and nightlife. [S1–S3]
- **Family business:** The family previously operated a household/baking-supplies shop; Eli transformed the business and his daughters **Moran** and **Yaara** became involved in the café. Moran trained in pastry arts in Paris. [S4, S5, S6]
- **Other initiative:** Associated with the revival of **Casino de Paris** in the market. [S3, S7]
- **Café closure:** Café Mizrahi closed in **September 2016**, after more than 14 years. [S5]
- **Public recognition:** Lit a torch during Israel's **69th Independence Day** ceremony (2017), as reported by media coverage. [S3, S8]

## 4. Market and social significance — keep the voices distinct

**Documented:** The café opened when Mahane Yehuda was primarily a daytime shopping district, and coverage connects it to a wider transformation into a cultural and hospitality destination. Family involvement, culinary ambition and retail adaptation are directly reported. [S1, S2, S4, S6]

**Attributed assessment:** Tali Friedman, Mayor Moshe Lion and others described Eli Mizrahi as a pioneer or catalyst. Record these as the speakers' assessments. [S1–S3]

**Additional historical nuance:** Later reporting acknowledges competing memories of *when* the market transformation began and notes the costs of commercialization and new chains. This is useful for DORI: the story is not simply that the old market was replaced by something better. [S10]

**Possible DORI interpretive idea — not fact:** A community's identity may become overlooked even while everyday life continues. Mizrahi's café can help explore how a place acquires new uses and audiences while older histories and livelihoods remain. This is an editorial question, not a quote attributed to Eli Mizrahi.
