# THE MEMORY PROTOCOL
*They rewrote the past to create peace. Instead, they created chaos.*

### The Story

- **The Discovery:** In the near future, a quantum breakthrough allows scientists to observe a parallel Earth, almost identical to ours, where humanity has suffered centuries of wars over land, resources, religion, and identity.
- **The Visitors:** An advanced extraterrestrial civilization called the **Israelianes** arrives from a distant galaxy. Having achieved extraordinary harmony on their home planet, they are horrified by humanity's endless conflicts and decide to help.
- **The Great Reset:** Believing that memories of hatred and conflict are the root cause of war, the Israelianes activate **HARMONY**, a powerful quantum system aboard their spacecraft. In one worldwide transmission, it rewrites human memory and historical records, replacing wars, violence, injustice, and suffering with a fabricated history in which humanity has always lived in perfect peace.
- **The Illusion:** At first, the intervention seems successful. People believe that peaceful coexistence has always been natural and effortless. But when new disagreements inevitably arise, humanity has no historical experience of dealing with conflict or learning from past mistakes.
- **The Catastrophe:** Conflicts escalate into even greater violence. The Israelianes realize their devastating mistake: they removed the memory of conflict, but not the human capacity to create it.
- **The Last Hope:** Scientists in our universe discover that authentic fragments of the parallel Earth's history can be reconstructed by comparing surviving evidence with records from our own world. Their investigation begins in Jerusalem, at the National Library of Israel, where historical documents connect to real buildings, neighborhoods, people, and traditions.

### The Mission

**You are the Memory Keepers.** Your mission is to recover authentic historical evidence from the National Library, record its identifying codes, and follow the clues through Jerusalem's neighborhoods and Mahane Yehuda Market. At each location, uncover the real story, verify the evidence, and restore another fragment of humanity's lost memory.

### The Moral

**Peace is not achieved by forgetting our differences or erasing painful history. It is built by understanding one another and learning from the past.**

### The Final Human Connection

At the end of the game, each teenager asks a parent or grandparent:

*"What is one story from your life or our family's past that you would want future generations to remember?"*

That story becomes the final memory recovered.
