### Critique (`/experience-design` step 8)

- **Better than the old stop:**
  - one mechanic becomes five forms;
  - the group needs each other in part 2;
  - the empty reveal now carries the stop's meaning: the AHA.
- **Screen time:** parts 2 and 3 put a phone in the Researcher's hand. Part 2 is written as a conversation (one phone, many eyes on the wall) to limit it. Parts 1 and 4a happen at the wall.
- **Quiz after every story:** four scored questions in a row risk it. They're broken up by different forms and by the spoken AHA, which carries no score.
- **Disguised test:** none. No answer is in a text they've just read: the name is on the wall, the price on a page, the real items on the wall, the technique's name after the experience.
- **Hotel-room test:** fails only for part 3 (see above).
- **Length:** see "The stop at a glance"; the owner decides Main or Optional.
