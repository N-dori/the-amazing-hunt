## 1. AUTHORITATIVE STORY — preserve the project V1 file exactly
The canonical, user-approved story is in Project source **`The_Memory_Protocol_Story_V1.md`**. **Do not silently rewrite it**. Compact continuity reminders:
- A quantum breakthrough allows observation of a parallel Earth.
- Peace-seeking aliens called **Israelianes**, from a harmonious civilization, activate quantum system **HARMONY**.
- HARMONY **rewrites people's memory AND the historical record to remove war, conflict, suffering and injustice, replacing them with a fabricated past of perfect peace**. It does *not* wipe all identity or erase history wholesale.
- People retain differences and capacity for conflict; when new disagreements emerge, a society lacking truthful precedent and learned lessons cannot manage them. Violence worsens; the Israelianes realize their mistake.
- Memory Keepers on our Earth reconstruct truthful history by comparing sources with parallel-world distortions, starting at the National Library of Israel and then Jerusalem/Nahlaot/Mahane Yehuda.
- **Moral:** Peace is built by understanding one another and learning from history, not by erasing painful differences.
- Final human connection: each teenager asks a parent/grandparent what family memory should be carried forward; privacy and consent respected.
**Story authority:** do not rebrand the aliens as Harmonists or swap premise without explicit user approval. The canonical file is the full text; this section is a synopsis only.

## 2. CORE GAMEPLAY — current leading design
1. Short grounded factual Library introduction outside; fictional transmission activates the role.
2. Three teams (pairs) become Memory Keepers / *contemporary crisis resolvers*. Teams compete for investigation/puzzle points while collaborating to restore human historical understanding.
3. Library: preordered physical books and/or digital records. Each pair aims for **two confirmed source finds** (optional third bonus), giving ~6 essential sources shared among all teams, not 4–5 per pair by default. Actual counts depend on what is accessible.
4. While items are retrieved: 2–4 Library investigator-certification warm-ups using art and architecture. **Use companion document `Memory_Protocol_Library_Tasks_v02.md`.** They are research-skill checks, NOT restored historical episodes.
5. Library-source flow: FIND a specific catalog item → COPY bibliographic identifier → EXTRACT a verifiable text/visual fact → note provenance and source type → SAVE it into a shared evidence dossier. The identifier alone is *not* proof of on-site reading; combine with passage-dependent answer.
6. Walk/guide transition: a clue directs the family to a specific public heritage site. At arrival, a lead pair uses earlier evidence as the expert witness; historical story and exact uncertainties are reconstructed.
7. **Only at or near the site**, a fictional **contemporary parallel-world crisis** is normally revealed, linked to the historical *underlying human dilemma*, not necessarily identical circumstances. Teams identify stakeholders, trade-offs and propose a modern response; historical precedent inspires thinking, it does **not** supply a predetermined right solution.
8. HARMONY updates its model, restores a truthful record and acknowledges ambiguity. Next station or culinary reward opens. Occasional short transmissions advance HARMONY's learning arc.
9. Every major stop may have three layers: **easy observational/history task**, **crisis reasoning (guide-evaluated rubric)**, **optional creative/math/visual puzzle (objective bonus)**. Time-based scoring only for safe bounded puzzles, not ethics. Keep comparable possible scores and equivalent accessible paths.
10. Final HARMONY reflection on constructive disagreement and family oral-history close (opt-in, no automatic publishing).

## 3. LEARNING PRINCIPLES / EVIDENCE BOUNDARIES
- Factual historical answer vs interpretive argument vs fictional crisis are **separately labeled in app**. Source types: catalog/bibliographic, historical archival object, physical site observation, oral tradition/testimony, modern business evidence, literary fiction.
- Fictional novel *The Beauty Queen of Jerusalem* can document how the author portrays Judeo-Spanish family cooking; characters/events are not automatically historical facts. Fiction and real Armoza family archive must not be conflated.
- Guide-told market and Nachlaot anecdotes remain attributed local stories until corroborated; don't invent historical dates or causal claims.
- Source/photo may not depict what description mentions; confirm actual visual contents before posing visual tasks.
- In app: **one idea, one question, one action, one reveal at a time**. DORI voice: restrained, warm, editorial; no neon SF dashboard; use approved branding from brand book when moving to visuals.
- DORI method source **`DORI Content Methodology.md`** separates treatment, interaction atom, question, source material, guide move, stance, constraint and renderer. Preserve that separation. Media formats (text, audio, video, camera) don't in themselves create learning.

## 5. LIBRARY LOGISTICS & FALLBACKS
- **Preferred:** order confirmed physical books in advance, to the correct reading-hall desk. **Pending:** complete warm-ups or short optional café break while waiting for email. **Unavailable:** pretested digital record, approved excerpt, alternate copy or alternate source serving identical learning goal. A digital *catalog record* is not necessarily full digital content. Some content works only on Library network.
- Most robotic-storage requests can take ~30–60 minutes; remote-storage ones may take **up to 2 working days**. Never promise retrieval on demand.
- Library currently publishes internally inconsistent caps/holds: Hebrew guidance describes max **15** and **7-day** pre-pickup hold in one section while another section mentions 14 days; English page mentions 20 and 15 in different paragraphs. Get confirmation from account, email and desk for intended date. https://www.nli.org.il/he/at-your-service/viewing-library-items/online-order and https://www.nli.org.il/en/at-your-service/reference/view-and-order-items
- Each reading-hall user needs an individual card; organized groups require permission. Splitting six into pairs is not a way to evade the group rule. Check whether account owner can distribute volumes and whether a study room is appropriate. https://www.nli.org.il/en/at-your-service/services/codes-of-conduct
- Special archival items require different authorization and possible advance ordering; catalog existence does NOT mean original is in the National Library building.
- **Rotunda / robotic storage window**: guide-only entry under user's official tour document. Optional, never mandatory for independent pair missions.
- Paid *Treasures* exhibit excluded from default free route; artwork encounters should occur only in publicly permitted zones. Hostage remembrance exhibit is not a timed/competitive puzzle.
- Recording inside building: reconnect to Library Wi-Fi and open digital viewer; test on actual device, permission and rights. Do not download/embed copyrighted recordings automatically.
- Library self-guided guide: https://guides.bloombergconnects.org/en-US/guide/nationalLibraryisrael ; official description https://www.nli.org.il/en/visit/bloomberg

## 7. SITE MISSIONS UNDER DEVELOPMENT
These are **story proposals**, not all historically verified, geographically routed or vendor booked.

**Hesed VeRahamim / Armoza:** Source describes an illicit drinking establishment in a residential context and conversion into Sephardic synagogue (1925), with later charity/community functions. A ten-lira purchase and dialogue are attributed traditions, not proven facts. Fictional crisis: shared building use/rights and different community needs. Evidence: archival description + site identification. Lesson: changing institutions and considering stakeholders, not “always replace a bar with a synagogue”. Next literature clue → borekitas.

**Borekitas / Beauty Queen:** Literature contains a family breakfast including cheese-filled borekitas; find exact page. Link to arranged borekas tasting, not guaranteed spontaneous vendor reward. Cultural memory and its transmission rather than proof that fictional family event occurred.

**Gan HaTut / water cistern:** historical shared water infrastructure; locate a safe, verified visible feature and compare to archival caption/image. Fictional contemporary crisis may be **an apartment building Shabbat-elevator expense/access dispute**, transferring shared-resource principles to a distinct situation. Include different religious/nonreligious/economic needs; if mentioning Israeli law, source check before presenting. Bonus classic mathematical **3L/5L containers to measure 4L** in six operations; digital puzzle, not pouring into historical cistern. Moral: fairness and infrastructure require negotiation, access and maintenance; history informs but cannot prescribe single solution.

**Rami Levy / HaShikma Street:** user's guide tells of early shop in 1976 and retail access to lower prices; physical sign candidate, verify onsite. User explicitly rejects a sugar-coated version. Explore **bulk purchasing and supply-chain power**: advantages to consumers and farmer stability alongside possible farmer dependence, bargaining asymmetry and competition effects. **Do NOT attribute specific coercive tomato contracts or market-wide price causation to Rami Levy without source evidence.** Fictional crisis: a big buyer promises to purchase entire tomato crop at lower prices, farmers weigh risk and dependency, retailers face competition. Puzzle: compare revenue under wholesale buyer scenarios, then separate risk/equity judgment. Moral: innovation has winners, costs and trade-offs.

**Mahane Yehuda market changes:** Compare dated market photo with present-day variety of produce stalls, prepared-food venues and visitor-facing businesses. Fictional crisis: thriving market but basic food is less affordable to locals; identify trade-offs, not assume historical causal mechanism. Evidence: archival photograph + dated provenance + documented real-world observation.

**Rabbi Aryeh Levin:** choose exactly sourced, attributed compassion episode; fictive crisis concerning unfair judgment of another's beliefs/dress. Avoid claiming any single anecdote is independently documented unless verified.

**Montefiore:** identify a particular documented plaque/name; charity and investment create benefits and governance questions. Historic spelling or inscription must be field tested.

**Ohel Moshe / Navon / Ladino / Gan HaTut:** language, family/community memory, water and shared courtyard; historical material is mixed institutional record and literary representation.

**Batei Goral / Yemenite heritage:** candidate site and source; no final physical task or exact record verified. May link to arranged malawach taste.

**Picture in Stone:** find one actual, legible family image with permitted display and identification; avoid claiming a fictional Mazalika character is a photographed historical person.

**Rahamim / charitable merchant:** guide oral tradition, identity and operating stall must be checked. Contemporary crisis: sustaining food assistance; optional consenting testimony. No unconsented photograph/customer questioning.
